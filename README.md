# SKYNZ — Creator Pages & Creator Control Center

**Your page. Your vibe. Your stage.**

**Status: working prototype / limited-beta preparation (reviewed November 2026).** SKYNZ is being developed as a configurable creator page and private control workspace with music/video embeds, social links, page styling, optional AI assistance, and future creator-page ownership records. This repository holds marketing and concept materials; it is **not** a verified production deployment.

## What exists in development

- A React/Express/PostgreSQL creator platform is committed in [SKYNZ-2.0](https://github.com/Justelliott6/SKYNZ-2.0), including registration/login routes, creator profiles, page CRUD, media/social-link UI, basic chat, and a page-analytics prototype. End-to-end reliability, hosting, privacy/security, and Windows deployment still need acceptance testing.
- Development branches include a side-by-side page editor and live preview, dual supported Spotify/YouTube embed slots, improved page persistence and owner-scoped edit routes, theme presets, and an authenticated Ollama-backed AI page-draft assistant. Branch existence does not mean merged or deployed.
- Standalone creator tools and studio modules are prototypes at different verification stages. They are not all integrated or production ready.
- The current visual references include luxury/dark and pink/glam creator-page examples. These are design targets, not evidence every depicted metric, player, or button is operational.

## The beta SKYNZ intends to ship

1. A free creator page with stable ID/handle, working preview, save/reopen/edit/publish and functional media/social links.
2. A creator-only workspace and AI-assisted **suggestions** that the creator reviews before applying and saving.
3. Small invited-user testing of registration, permissions, persistence, mobile usability, exports/backups, and incident recovery.
4. A separate Solana **devnet** test for creator-page ownership, using Metaplex Core after implementation and independent transaction/ownership verification.
5. **Optional paid minting later** after a reviewed price, technical/security tests and creator approval. The original free-beta page and content should remain intact if its creator upgrades.

The initial beta does **not** require an immersive 3D/game interface. Game-style Creator Forge and optional premium presentation experiences remain future work.

## Not yet verified or available as a production promise

- Paid creator-page NFT minting, wallet ownership synchronization, transfers, marketplace sales, royalties, revenue sharing, or passive-income payouts.
- Audited smart contracts, guaranteed royalty enforcement, automatic payments, guaranteed creator revenue percentages, or specific resale prices.
- Live multi-platform broadcast orchestration, unlimited streaming, advanced audience analytics, or AI agents acting without explicit creator authorization.
- A fixed $199 price, unlimited premium access, specific support response times, an active payment checkout, or a confirmed release date.

**No NFT or token currently guarantees website hosting, utility, revenue, or a royalty by itself.** Any token-gated feature needs actual SKYNZ application enforcement; terms, transfer policy and fees must be published and tested.

## Design and technical principle

The SKYNZ platform—not the language model—owns identity, state, permission checks, tools and audit history. Models and standalone apps must remain replaceable, with isolated failure modes and human approval for consequential actions.
